\(\text{Probe: } l = \dfrac{R \cdot A}{\rho} = \dfrac{0.453\;\Omega \cdot 1.5\;\text{mm}^2}{0.017\;\Omega\,\text{mm}^2/\text{m}} \approx 40\;\text{m}\)
